How to get 7 band in ielts in 10 days?

To get a Band 7 in IELTS in 10 days, you must intensely practice all sections, focusing on understanding the test format and identifying your weaknesses. Daily immersion in English is crucial, including listening to news, reading articles, and actively speaking with a focus on fluency, confidence, and varied vocabulary and grammar. Record and evaluate your speaking, get feedback on writing, and do mock tests to pinpoint areas needing improvement for the final few days of review and rest.
Daily Routine & Focus
Day 1:
Familiarize yourself with the test format and criteria for each section.
Days 2-5:
Rotate focus on each section: Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking.
Listening: Practice by listening to English news (e.g., BBC) and podcasts.
Reading: Read diverse news items and articles to build your knowledge and speed.
Writing: Learn essay structures and practice writing daily, getting feedback if possible.
Speaking: Record yourself speaking, focus on avoiding filler words, and practice expanding your answers.
Day 6:
Take a full mock IELTS test under timed conditions to identify weaknesses.
Days 7-8:
Review the mock test results, focusing your practice on your weakest areas.
Days 9-10:
Focus on overall review and rest.
Key Strategies for Each Section
Speaking:
Speak confidently and fluently, avoiding hesitation and filler words like "um".
Use a range of vocabulary and grammatical structures.
Record yourself to check pronunciation, fluency, and sentence structure.
Practice answering questions with a partner or by talking to yourself in the mirror.
Listening:
Concentrate on understanding the conversation and following instructions.
Practice avoiding spelling mistakes.
Reading:
Practice under timed conditions to improve speed and accuracy.
Read widely to develop familiarity with topics and gain a broad vocabulary.
Writing:
Structure your essays logically.
Use a range of complex grammatical structures and less common vocabulary, but focus on minimizing errors.
Use linking words to connect ideas effectively.
General Tips
Immerse yourself in English:
Think in English, even in your daily routines.
Use authentic materials:
Utilize resources from IELTS websites like Take IELTS and IELTS Liz.
Seek feedback:
Get an experienced trainer to review your writing and speaking to help identify your mistakes.
