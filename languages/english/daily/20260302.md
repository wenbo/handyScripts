# First Principles

## ロバート・ディルツ（Robert Dilts）
神経言語プログラミング(NLP)分野の主要な開発者であり、
エキスパートとして、ロバートは世界中の個人と組織に
コーチング、コンサルティング、トレーニングを幅広く提供しています。


Everyone must remember **that** the first principle of making money is **to create** value, not **to** sell time.

The first principle of creating value is **to solve** problems, not **to** provide labor.

The first principle of solving problems is **to identify** real needs, **gather** feedback quickly, and then iterate quickly.

The first principle of fast iteration is **to verify** the model **in order to** replicate the system better.

The first principle of system replication is **leveraged** thinking. Only by using **leverage to amplify your** system can you really **move beyond manual labor** and move towards freedom.


Creating value by solving problems, rather than selling time, is a foundational principle for building sustainable wealth. By identifying real needs and using first-principles thinking to break down complex issues, one can innovate and create scalable solutions. This approach shifts focus from labor to value creation.

Key Principles of Value Creation & Wealth
Solve Problems, Don't Just Sell Time: The highest form of value creation is addressing a need or solving a pain point, which provides a better ROI than trading hours for money.
First Principles Thinking: This involves breaking down complex problems into their most basic, fundamental truths to rebuild, innovate, and find solutions from scratch rather than following existing, often inefficient methods.
Identify Real Needs: Value is created by addressing authentic problems discovered through practice and iteration, not by working in isolation.
Leverage and Scalability: Successful value creation often involves using capital, technology, or systems that allow for replication and exponential growth rather than linear, time-based labor.
Rapid Iteration: Validating models quickly through feedback and testing ensures solutions are effective and valuable.
This mindset is famously applied by entrepreneurs like Elon Musk, who broke down the cost of rocket components rather than accepting high industry prices.


By the age of 46 years old, Elon Musk has innovated and built three revolutionary multibillion dollar companies in completely different fields — Paypal (Financial Services), Tesla Motors (Automotive) and SpaceX (Aerospace).
